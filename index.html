About the Media Unit

The Media Unit of the District Secretariat, Jaffna plays an important role in strengthening communication between the District Secretariat, government institutions, media organizations and the general public. The Unit facilitates the dissemination of accurate and timely information regarding government programmes, development activities, public services, important events and initiatives implemented within the Jaffna District. District Media Units have been established under the Department of Government Information to function as important centres for collecting development-related information from districts and sharing such information with media organizations. The Jaffna District Media Unit has also contributed to documenting and communicating the socio-economic development, cultural activities, historical resources and major development initiatives of the Jaffna District.

✦

Vision & Mission

Vision

To strengthen effective, accurate and timely communication between the District Secretariat, government institutions, media organizations and the people of Jaffna District.

Mission

To collect, verify, document and disseminate information on government activities, development programmes, public events and important initiatives in Jaffna District through appropriate media and communication channels.

✦

Main Objectivities

The main objectives of the Media Unit are:

  • To provide accurate and timely information to the public through appropriate media channels.
  • To publicize government development programmes and public services implemented in Jaffna District.
  • To strengthen coordination between the District Secretariat and media organizations.
  • To facilitate media coverage of important government events and official programmes.
  • To collect and maintain information relating to development activities and significant events in the district.
  • To promote awareness of government programmes, policies and services among the public.
  • To document important cultural, social, historical and development activities of Jaffna District.
  • To support effective communication between government institutions and the media community.
